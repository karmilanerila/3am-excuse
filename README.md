A hardware wallet refuses to sign a transaction at 3 AM — in one line.

`main.py` writes the wallet's excuse to `/output/entry.txt`: a device that
remembers the last time its owner woke it at 3 AM (to buy a JPEG of a rock)
and has decided it is not signing anything until sunrise.
